# MoveMail: Two-Minute Live Demo

## The one-line story

A family member sends a personal movement postcard. AI turns the memory into
three gentle movements from a fixed, product-owned library. The recipient
completes a seated camera game designed to take about 60 seconds, scaled to
their comfortable reach, to unlock the message.

## Set-up before the clock starts

- Open MoveMail on the creation screen with the built-in Brighton example.
- Use a steady chair about 1.5 metres from the camera.
- Run the camera route once, then press **Start over**. This leaves permission
  granted and the pose assets warm before the clock starts.
- Keep sound on. If ElevenLabs is configured, confirm one instruction plays.
- Keep the share link and camera-free controls visible as fallbacks, but use the
  camera route for the main demonstration.
- Do not type during the judged demo; the example is already filled in.

## What the judges should remember

**Personal message → bounded AI plan → comfortable camera movement → emotional
reward.**

The AI adds personalisation, but the product—not the model—owns the movement
vocabulary, physical cues, validation and fallback behaviour.

In this demo, “approved movements” means movement mechanics on that
product-owned allow-list. It does not mean clinically approved.

## Recovery lines

- **If the AI is slow:** “After a bounded timeout, the built-in validated
  sequence takes over.”
- **If camera permission fails:** select **Use on-screen controls** and say,
  “Camera is optional; the same postcard remains usable without it.”
- **If ElevenLabs is unavailable:** “Visible instructions and browser speech
  take over automatically.” Say **ElevenLabs** during the main demo only when
  the final receipt confirms **ElevenLabs narration**.
- **If time is running out:** skip the comfort check, complete the three
  on-screen controls, and spend the final ten seconds on the revealed message.

Do not claim medical benefit, fall prevention, accessibility conformance or
older-adult validation. No older-adult user evidence has yet been recorded.
